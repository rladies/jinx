A few things worth knowing:

Hold your first event within the next six months, then keep the chapter
active with at least one event every six months. Many chapters run
something every two or three months. If that frequency turns out to be
hard, tell us rather than going quiet - we would much rather help than
retire a chapter.

Publish every event on Meetup. That is what counts as activity, and it is
how members find you.

Use the chapter email address to create any social accounts, and cc it on
replies so your co-organisers can see them. Keep its password in a
password manager rather than a document or a chat message, turn on
two-factor authentication, and save the recovery codes somewhere you will
still have them if you lose your phone.

The organisers' guide has the rest, including how to run events and how to
add co-organisers: https://guide.rladies.org/
